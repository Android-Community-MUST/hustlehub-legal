HustleHub Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 19, 2026
Last Updated: September 19, 2026
Platform: Android Application and Web Services

Welcome to HustleHub ("we", "our", or "us"). HustleHub is a native Android campus peer-to-peer service marketplace built specifically for students and community members of Meru University of Science and Technology (MUST).

Your privacy and data security are central to everything we build. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect when you use the HustleHub Android application and associated services, how your information is encrypted and protected, why it is processed, and your rights under applicable data protection principles and Google Play Developer Policies.

Google Play Data Safety Compliance: HustleHub adheres strictly to Google Play's User Data and Privacy policies. We collect only the data necessary to provide core marketplace functionality, never sell your personal information, provide hardware-backed end-to-end encryption for in-app chat, and provide clear mechanisms to delete your account both in-app and via the web.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the data required to facilitate secure peer-to-peer campus services:

  • Account and Identity Information: Your full name, official university email address (restricted to @must.ac.ke domains), phone number, and student verification status.
  • Profile Information: Display name, profile avatar photo, student bio, and skills or service tags that you choose to display publicly on your provider profile. We do not collect or store your graduation year or course/department.
  • Service Listings and Portfolio Media: Service titles, detailed descriptions, category selections, pricing ranges, availability status (Available, Busy, Offline), and portfolio images uploaded to showcase your work.
  • Communications and Messages: Text messages, timestamps, and conversation metadata exchanged between buyers and service providers via in-app chat. All chat messages are end-to-end encrypted (E2EE) on-device prior to transmission.
  • Location Information: With your explicit runtime permission, campus location coordinates or approximate campus zones (such as Main Campus gates or hostels) to display nearby service providers on the interactive campus map. You can disable location sharing at any time in your device or app privacy settings.
  • Ratings and Reviews: Star ratings, written feedback, and review timestamps submitted following completed service engagements.
  • Device and Diagnostic Information: Device model, Android OS version, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device registration tokens (used solely to deliver push notifications for incoming messages and service alerts), and anonymized crash diagnostics.

2. End-to-End Encryption (E2EE) Architecture for Communications

We believe that private communications between students should remain private. HustleHub implements a zero-knowledge, client-side encryption architecture for in-app messaging:

  • Cryptographic Primitives: In-app chat utilizes Elliptic Curve Diffie-Hellman (ECDH) over the NIST P-256 curve for cryptographic key agreement, paired with authenticated AES-256-GCM symmetric encryption for message content.
  • Hardware-Backed Key Storage: Private cryptographic keys are generated directly on your device and stored securely within the hardware-backed AndroidKeyStore (Hardware Security Module / Trusted Execution Environment). Private keys never leave your device and are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Zero-Knowledge Server Relay: The HustleHub backend server acts strictly as an untrusted message relay and stores only ciphertext blobs, initialization vectors, and authentication tags. Server administrators and backend databases cannot decrypt or view your messages.
  • Notification Privacy: Push notification previews delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging display a generic encrypted placeholder so third-party push notification relays never receive plaintext message content.

3. Why We Collect Your Data

Your information is processed strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Campus Authentication: Verifying your @must.ac.ke university email to maintain an exclusive, trusted campus environment.
  • Marketplace Discovery: Powering category filtering, campus map discovery, and natural language search (facilitated server-side via the Gemini AI API to interpret student queries such as "braids near Gate B").
  • Service Fulfillment: Enabling service providers and clients to coordinate schedules, scope work, and agree on pricing.
  • Push Notifications: Informing you promptly of new chat messages, service requests, and platform announcements.
  • Safety and Abuse Prevention: Detecting fraudulent listings, enforcing our Code of Conduct, and investigating reported safety violations within the campus community.

4. Data Storage and Infrastructure Architecture

HustleHub utilizes a modern, secure infrastructure stack:

  • Application Data: All user profiles, service listings, reviews, and encrypted message ciphertext are stored in an enterprise PostgreSQL database managed through an authenticated Spring Boot REST API. We do not use Cloud Firestore, Firebase Realtime Database, or Supabase for application data.
  • Identity Authentication: User authentication and login credential verification are handled securely via Firebase Authentication using industry-standard tokens (JSON Web Tokens).
  • Push Notifications: Message alerts and updates are routed via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
  • Media Storage: Portfolio and profile images are uploaded via multipart requests to our Spring Boot backend and stored in a secure cloud bucket.
  • In-Transit Encryption: All network traffic between the Android application and our backend endpoints is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) and secure WebSockets (WSS).

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

Strict No-Sale Policy: HustleHub does not sell, lease, rent, or trade student personal data to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks under any circumstances.

Data is shared only in the following limited contexts:

  • Campus Community: Your public profile (name, avatar, bio, skills) and published service listings are visible to other verified MUST students on the platform.
  • Infrastructure Providers: Cloud hosting, database, and notification providers (Google Firebase and backend cloud infrastructure) who process data strictly on our behalf under confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal and University Administration: When required by Kenyan law, legal process, or university authorities to investigate serious fraud, physical safety threats, or gross violations of university regulations.

6. User Rights and Data Controls

You have comprehensive control over your personal data:

  • Access and Correction: You can view and update your profile information, contact details, and service listings directly within the app at any time.
  • Privacy Settings: You can adjust location visibility and messaging permissions via Settings → Privacy Settings.
  • Data Portability: You may request an export of your account data by contacting our team via email.
  • Account and Data Deletion: You have the absolute right to delete your account and all associated personal data permanently, either within the app or through our web portal.

7. Account Deletion and Retention Policy

In accordance with Google Play requirements, users can request permanent deletion of their account and data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Available in the Android app under Settings → Delete Account. Confirmation requires password verification and takes effect immediately.
  • Web Deletion: Available for users who have uninstalled the app via our Account Deletion Portal. Web requests are verified and processed within 30 days.

Upon deletion, your profile, service listings, reviews, and bookmarks are permanently purged. Anonymized security logs and statutory financial records (if official Paybill transactions were made) may be retained for legally mandated audit periods.

8. Children's Privacy

HustleHub is intended exclusively for university students and adult members of the Meru University community. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18 years of age.
