HustleHub Account and Data Deletion

Policy Type: Google Play Data Safety Compliance
Processing Time: Immediate (In-App) or within 30 Days (Web)

At HustleHub, we respect your privacy and ownership of your personal data. In compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, we provide both an instant in-app deletion option and this public web deletion portal for users who have uninstalled the app or cannot access their mobile device.

Permanent Action Notice: Deleting your account is permanent. Once completed, your profile, active service listings, chat history, and bookmarks cannot be recovered.

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended - Instant)

If you have the HustleHub Android app installed, you can delete your account immediately:

  1. Open HustleHub on your Android phone and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar, then tap the Settings (gear icon).
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the Settings screen to the Account section.
  4. Tap the red "Delete Account" button.
  5. Read the confirmation dialog, enter your current password to verify ownership, and tap "Delete My Account".

Your account, session, and associated personal data will be immediately purged from our database and Firebase Authentication.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you no longer have access to the HustleHub Android app, you can submit an account deletion request directly via email. We will process your request within 30 days after verifying account ownership.

Data Deletion and Retention Breakdown

Here is an exact breakdown of how your data is handled upon account deletion:

Permanently Deleted

  • Full user profile (name, email, phone number, bio, avatar)
  • All posted service listings, descriptions, and uploaded portfolio photos
  • In-app chat messages, message ciphertext, and conversation history
  • Saved bookmarks and favorites
  • FCM push notification tokens and device credentials
  • Firebase Authentication credentials and session tokens

Retained Data (If Applicable)

  • Financial and Transaction Records: If an official Paybill or subscription transaction was made, transaction reference IDs are retained for statutory accounting, tax, and fraud prevention as required by Kenyan law.
  • Security Audit Logs: Anonymized server access logs without personal identifiers, retained for security and attack prevention for up to 90 days.
